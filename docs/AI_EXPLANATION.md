# ReFind AI — Artificial Intelligence & Matching Methodology

This document explains the Natural Language Processing (NLP) pipeline, embedding model, mathematical scoring formula, and Explainable AI (XAI) design implemented in [`ai_matcher.py`](../ai_matcher.py).

---

## 1. Why Keyword Matching Fails for Lost & Found

Traditional search tools look for exact character or word overlaps. Consider two real-world campus reports:

- **Lost:** *"My blue water bottle is missing from the classroom."*
- **Found:** *"Found a navy reusable drinking bottle in Room 204."*

Only a single content word (`bottle`) overlaps verbatim. A keyword system scores this pair poorly even though any human immediately recognizes `blue` ≈ `navy`, `water bottle` ≈ `drinking bottle`, and `classroom` ≈ `Room 204`.

ReFind AI solves this using **dense semantic embeddings** generated by a pretrained Transformer model.

---

## 2. Pretrained Transformer Model

- **Model:** `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`
- **Family:** MiniLM (distilled 6-layer Transformer encoder, trained via contrastive Siamese learning on over 1 billion sentence pairs)
- **Output:** A 384-dimensional floating-point vector ($\mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^{384}$) representing the semantic meaning of the item text.
- **Execution:** Runs 100% locally on a standard laptop CPU with no paid APIs or external keys. In Streamlit (`app.py`), the model is loaded **once** using `@st.cache_resource` so repeated queries stay fast and responsive.

### Error Handling Policy
If the model cannot be downloaded (e.g., offline first run) or fails to initialize, `ai_matcher.py` raises an explicit `ModelLoadError` and surfaces actionable guidance in the UI. **ReFind AI never silently falls back to keyword matching**, preserving the integrity of the genuine AI pipeline.

---

## 3. Text Preprocessing & Embedding Pipeline

For every report, `build_item_text()` combines the available descriptive fields into a structured natural-language string:

```text
<title>. <description>. <additional_details (if provided)>
```

Whitespace is normalized and empty fields are handled gracefully without crashing. Each cleaned text string is encoded by `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` into a 384-D vector. Computed embeddings are cached in memory by normalized text so browsing or re-ranking candidates avoids redundant neural forward passes.

---

## 4. Cosine Similarity Calculation

Given a source report embedding $\mathbf{u} \in \mathbb{R}^{384}$ and a candidate opposite-type report embedding $\mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^{384}$, ReFind AI computes pairwise cosine similarity using `sklearn.metrics.pairwise.cosine_similarity`:

$$\cos(\theta) = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\|_2 \, \|\mathbf{v}\|_2} \in [-1, 1]$$

For hybrid scoring, the cosine similarity is clamped to $[0.0, 1.0]$:

$$S_{\text{sem}} = \max(0.0, \min(1.0, \cos(\theta)))$$

Negative cosine similarities (indicating unrelated or opposing semantics) contribute `0.0` rather than distorting the positive weighted scale. Both the bounded score (`semantic_score`) and raw cosine value (`raw_cosine_similarity`) are preserved in `MatchResult`.

---

## 5. Hybrid Contextual Scoring Formula

Semantic text similarity carries the majority of the decision weight, complemented by three independent, transparent contextual signals:

| Signal | Weight | Computation | Range |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Semantic Similarity** ($S_{\text{sem}}$) | **70%** (`0.70`) | Clamped cosine similarity between `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` embeddings | `[0.0, 1.0]` |
| **Category Match** ($S_{\text{cat}}$) | **15%** (`0.15`) | `1.0` if normalized categories match exactly, else `0.0` | `{0.0, 1.0}` |
| **Location Match** ($S_{\text{loc}}$) | **10%** (`0.10`) | Transparent normalized string & token overlap (no invented GIS distances) | `[0.0, 1.0]` |
| **Date Proximity** ($S_{\text{date}}$) | **5%** (`0.05`) | Exponential decay: $\exp(-\Delta d / 14)$ where $\Delta d$ is days apart | `[0.0, 1.0]` |

### Final Normalized Score (0–100)

$$\text{Final Score} = 100 \times \Big(0.70 \cdot S_{\text{sem}} + 0.15 \cdot S_{\text{cat}} + 0.10 \cdot S_{\text{loc}} + 0.05 \cdot S_{\text{date}}\Big)$$

### Details on Contextual Signals

1. **Category Match (`compute_category_score`):** Lowercases and trims both category strings. Returns `1.0` on exact match and `0.0` otherwise.
2. **Location Match (`compute_location_score`):**
   - Lowercases and removes punctuation from both location strings.
   - Exact match → `1.0` (`exact`).
   - Direct substring containment (e.g., `"library"` in `"central library"`) → `0.85` (`substring`).
   - Otherwise strips generic spatial prepositions (`near`, `outside`, `inside`, `at`, `in`, `the`, `by`, `around`, `front`, `of`, `next`, `to`) and computes Jaccard similarity $\frac{|A \cap B|}{|A \cup B|}$ over meaningful tokens (`token_overlap` if > 0, else `different`).
   - Importantly, this method never invents physical distances between buildings it does not know.
3. **Date Proximity (`compute_date_proximity_score`):**
   - Parses ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` dates and calculates $\Delta d = |\text{date}_A - \text{date}_B|$.
   - Computes $S_{\text{date}} = e^{-\Delta d / \tau}$ with default $\tau = 14$ days:
     - 0 days apart → `1.00`
     - 1 day apart → `0.93`
     - 3 days apart → `0.81`
     - 7 days apart → `0.61`
     - 14 days apart → `0.37`
     - 30 days apart → `0.12`
   - Missing or invalid dates safely return `0.0`.

All weights and thresholds are configurable via `MatchConfig` in `ai_matcher.py` and adjustable live in the AI Matching Dashboard.

---

## 6. Opposite Report-Type Filtering

Before ranking, `find_matches()` strictly enforces opposite report types:
- A **`lost`** report is compared **only** against **`found`** reports.
- A **`found`** report is compared **only** against **`lost`** reports.
- The source report's own `id` is always excluded.
- By default, only **`active`** candidates are matched (preventing already-recovered items from cluttering active recommendations).

---

## 7. Explainable AI (XAI) Rationale Generation

`generate_match_explanation()` produces a plain-language explanation built **strictly from measured evidence**:

- **Semantic alignment summary:** Categorizes $S_{\text{sem}}$ into Strong ($\ge 0.70$), Moderate ($\ge 0.45$), or Low ($< 0.45$) and reports the exact semantic percentage.
- **Category alignment:** Explicitly states whether both reports belong to the same category or different categories.
- **Location alignment:** States whether locations match identically, share overlapping campus terms (naming the shared tokens when available), or differ.
- **Date proximity:** States the exact calendar gap (e.g., *"reported on the same day"*, *"reported 1 day apart"*, or *"reported 12 days apart"*).

The system never claims visual similarity from images, physical proximity between unlinked rooms, or facts outside the evaluated fields.

---

## 8. Similarity Score vs. Probability (Academic Note)

A critical distinction for academic evaluation:

> **A score of 85% is a relative similarity score—NOT an 85% probability that two reports describe the same physical item.**

- **Similarity score:** A deterministic weighted combination of vector cosine similarity and contextual heuristics bounded to `[0, 100]`. It is designed to **rank** candidates from most plausible to least plausible.
- **Probability:** A calibrated statistical estimate $P(Y = 1 \mid X)$ derived from labeled positive/negative training outcomes (e.g., via Platt scaling or logistic regression on historical verified reunions).

Because ReFind AI operates out-of-the-box without a labeled historical campus dataset, its scores represent **ranked similarity evidence** to assist human judgment, not calibrated probabilities.
